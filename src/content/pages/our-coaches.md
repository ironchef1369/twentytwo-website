## Our program owners

Team 22 is led by Brandon Holmes and Jennifer Holmes. Both are former Division I basketball players who bring a love of the game and a commitment to developing well-rounded student-athletes.

<div class="owner-profile">
<img src="/assets/brandon-holmes-headshot.webp" alt="Brandon Holmes, Coach B" width="800" height="800" loading="lazy" />
<div>

### Brandon Holmes · Coach B

**Program Owner**

Coach B played Division I college basketball and traveled overseas to play in various countries. His coaching experience includes the JSerra High School boys varsity staff, youth teams, skills training, and basketball camps. He has also trained NBA players and professionals who play overseas.

</div>
</div>

<div class="owner-profile">
<img src="/assets/jennifer-holmes-headshot.webp" alt="Jennifer Holmes, Coach Jenn" width="800" height="800" loading="lazy" />
<div>

### Jennifer Holmes · Coach Jenn

**Program Owner**

Coach Jenn played Division I women's basketball while earning her degree, following four years of high school varsity basketball. Her coaching experience includes girls varsity and JV basketball at Santa Margarita Catholic High School, youth teams, skills training, and basketball camps.

</div>
</div>

For more than 15 years, Coach B and Coach Jenn have coached young athletes in Orange County and surrounding communities. Together, they help players work toward their goals while building discipline, leadership, teamwork, and confidence.

## The purpose behind the coaching

We help players build the skills and habits to keep growing through each stage of the game. That means purposeful instruction, honest encouragement, and an expectation that players work, listen, and support their teammates.
