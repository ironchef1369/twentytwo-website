## Our home court

OC Hoop Legends is the home of TwentyTwo in Lake Forest. A dedicated environment gives players a consistent place to practice, build habits, and feel connected to the program.

The gym is where much of the journey happens: the repeated footwork, the new skill, the team conversation, and the next attempt after something does not go to plan.

## Built for the work

The facility includes NBA-style hardwood, climate control, video capability, and Dr. Dish training. Strength and conditioning is available next door.

Those tools support purposeful training. Our focus stays on how players use the space to develop their skills, understanding, movement, and confidence.
