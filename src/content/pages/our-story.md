## Meet Coach B and Coach Jenn

Team 22 was founded by Coach Brandon Holmes and Coach Jennifer Holmes. Both are former Division I basketball players who share a love of the game and a commitment to helping young athletes grow.

For more than 15 years, Coach B and Coach Jenn have coached youth players in Orange County and surrounding communities. Their experience includes high school coaching in the Trinity League, skills training, and youth basketball camps. Team 22 reflects their dedication to developing well-rounded student-athletes and building lasting relationships with players and families.

## Why TwentyTwo

Basketball gives young players a place to challenge themselves, make friends, and discover what they can do. We want that experience to lead somewhere: stronger skills, better decisions, lasting confidence, and a love of the game that keeps growing.

TwentyTwo is built around that longer view. A season is one chapter in a player's career. What a player learns, how they respond, and the people who support them matter well beyond the final game.

## A family around the player

Behind every athlete is someone making the early drive, rearranging a weekend, or listening after a hard game. Those families are part of our program, too.

Our aim is a community where players feel known and parents understand the purpose behind the work. We want families to share in the experience, encourage every teammate, and build relationships that last beyond basketball.

## What we're building

We bring skill development, basketball IQ, athletic development, character, and competition into one environment. Each part supports the others. Practice builds the tools; games give players a chance to use them; reflection helps them take the next step.

That journey leads from learning to enjoy the game toward preparing for high school basketball and whatever comes next. The destination will look different for each player. Our commitment to helping each player grow stays the same.
